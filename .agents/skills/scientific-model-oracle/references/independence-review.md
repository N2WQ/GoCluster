# Scientific Independence Review

Use this reference when the credibility of a scientific or model conclusion may
depend on reasoning outside the lead's implementation context.

## When Separate Review Is Required

Use a separate non-steered read-only reviewer when one or more of these
conditions applies:

* a consequential scientific or model contract is being created or materially
  changed;
* the lead has already formed or advocated a preferred interpretation;
* expected values, classifications, thresholds, or tolerances could be
  contaminated by implementation-derived reasoning;
* the claim affects prediction, safety, trust, ranking, classification,
  correction, probability, confidence, or externally visible scientific
  behavior;
* normative sources are disputed or materially uncertain;
* independent derivation is necessary for credible golden vectors;
* the credibility of the recommendation depends on showing that it was derived
  outside the implementation context.

Do not require separate review merely because scientific terminology appears.
Use it when independence materially strengthens the evidence.

## Neutral Evidence Packet

The reviewer may receive:

* the neutral scientific or model question;
* confirmed product objectives;
* operating domain;
* relevant variables and units;
* authoritative source candidates;
* current behavior only when clearly labeled as observational evidence;
* unresolved questions;
* known physical, mathematical, or operational constraints.

The reviewer must not receive:

* the lead's preferred answer;
* the intended implementation;
* a draft Scope Ledger;
* implementation-derived expected values;
* current golden vectors copied from tests or fixtures;
* a proposed threshold table presented as the expected result;
* wording designed to steer the reviewer toward the lead's conclusion.

## Reviewer Task

Ask the reviewer to:

1. derive the normative contract from the supplied evidence;
2. identify the controlling sources and conflicts;
3. define applicable units, domains, boundaries, tolerances, and uncertainty;
4. identify unsupported claims;
5. derive independent reference cases where possible;
6. state confidence and evidence gaps;
7. distinguish scientific conclusions from product-policy choices.

The reviewer remains findings-only and read-only.

## Lead Responsibilities

The lead agent must:

* verify material citations and derivations;
* compare independent findings with repository-grounded findings;
* identify material agreement and disagreement;
* disposition disagreements explicitly;
* reduce confidence when disagreement remains unresolved;
* retain all product-policy, scope, design, approval, implementation, and
  validation authority;
* avoid duplicating the complete independent investigation in the final answer.

Independent evidence does not transfer workflow authority.

## Required Disclosure

The final response must state:

* whether a separate non-steered reviewer was actually used;
* whether the result is independent, separately reviewed, or lead-owned;
* the neutral question and material evidence supplied;
* whether the reviewer saw the lead's preferred conclusion or intended design;
* the reviewer's material findings;
* material agreement or disagreement;
* the lead's disposition of disagreement;
* remaining uncertainty and confidence limits.

Do not describe any of the following as independent:

* a fresh lead pass;
* a second pass in the same inherited context;
* a reviewer shown the preferred answer;
* a reviewer shown implementation-derived expected values;
* a reviewer instructed to validate a predetermined conclusion.

## When Independent Context Is Unavailable

When required separate review cannot be obtained:

* disclose the limitation before issuing a verdict;
* identify claims exposed to correlated-reasoning risk;
* perform only a clearly labeled lead-owned derivation;
* reduce confidence where independence could matter;
* avoid claiming an independently established contract;
* state what separate evidence is still required;
* follow the Scientific Independence Gate before producing a verdict or Scope
  Ledger that depends on the missing independence.

A request to continue, review, recommend, plan, or propose is not acceptance of
the independence limitation.
