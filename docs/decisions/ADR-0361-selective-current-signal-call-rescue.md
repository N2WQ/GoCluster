# ADR-0361: Selective Current-Signal Call Rescue

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Decision Origin: Design and follow-up implementation; exact Approved v8

## Context

The ordinary resolver can miss a supported lower-ranked call when a different
signal dominates its frequency bucket. The v7 focal-observation ordinal ranking
changed trial order without changing applied outcomes on its exposed study.
Additional rescues also exposed credible competing identities. More proposals
therefore need bounded current evidence and permission to decline an addition
even when its numerical gates would allow it.

RBN archives are the available development data. Their reference labels and
follow-on stability are proxies, not RF truth. Frozen CTY representative inputs
remain conditional study geography. No calibrated RF probability is claimed.

## Decision

### Selective rescue C

Preserve the ordinary method and make accepted mutation, suppression and chosen
replacement rejection terminal through an explicit private outcome. An optional
`call_correction.selective_rescue_enabled` adds C only after nonterminal failure.
Omission/false preserves ordinary behavior; present null/nonboolean is invalid.
Ship false. False retains the old effective JSON shape; true changes its hash.
Raw source-file hashes remain authoritative.

C reuses current frequency association: up to five source reads, 80 raw spelling
slots, 5,120 incidences and 16 components. Use a 150-Hz component gap, unique
focal attachment within 100 Hz, and pairwise exclusion supported by two shared
receiver labels within 60 seconds. Unknown, spanning and unresolved evidence
remains dissent. These witnesses do not prove separate transmitters.

Nominate currently observed, source-authorized calls through configured CW
distance three intersected with the global cap. Global zero permits none;
negative global distance still leaves C capped at three. Preserve all ordinary
report, advantage, confidence, history, licensing, pending and loss restrictions,
including distance-three extras and comparable-neighbor restrictions for either
recent-plus-one or structurally enabled legacy Bayes modes.

Every retained identity, including non-nominees, participates in the competition
veto. Credibility requires two distinct positive-weight receiver labels in
weighted mode, or two labels in explicit raw mode. A credible identity outside
the supported alphabet or a canonical identity longer than 32 characters refuses
the whole C assessment before distance/tie helpers. A credible rival not related under existing
canonical/configured family policy declines a nominee when its directed
rival-observed to nominee-truth correction distance exceeds the active cap.
Related identities remain numerical dissent. This detects the specified
competition pattern; it does not eliminate the risk of renaming a real station.

Keep an eight-trial gate beam. The first gate allowance alone crosses final
replacement validation; rejection never authorizes another target. Accepted C
uses existing metadata/grid invalidation, final validation, confirmation and
delivery ownership. Its public percentage comes from ordinary evidence for the
actual emitted identity, or is unavailable. Group/model scores do not become
public confidence, and C does not become V. CustomSCP retains V-only learning;
legacy accepted CW/RTTY history retains its different admission rules.

### Temporal and retained ownership

Immediate CW and genuine held resolver fallback may assess fresh C. Temporal
Apply additionally requires the existing release guard's admitted identity and
the Observe-time five-source lifecycle vector. Reread current rows/counts/clocks
even when generations agree. Saved selections remain compact. Capture and
release use their actual decision clocks.

Only two masks and five uint64 generation values enter the existing bounded
pending owner. No receiver view, score, proposal, cache, worker, timer or new
state owner is retained. Terminal release keeps existing deletion. Temporal
abstain/bypass and every Observe overflow gain no extra rescue. All stabilizer
retries remain ordinary-only; action names and confidence glyphs cannot recover
earlier rescue authority.

Live admission remains the existing source-agnostic CW surface. RBN-only study
inputs do not add a live SourceRBN filter or prove mixed-source performance.

### Experimental receiver compatibility B

B is shared pure replay code, with no live policy or model-loader setting. Use
the existing frozen ordinal asset without refitting. All retained identities,
including zero-weight, non-nominee and C-disqualified identities, are opposing
hypotheses. Add canonical incoming KEEP once. Admit at most eight observed
identities and nine total hypotheses; overflow abstains without truncation.

Each hypothesis sees the same positive canonical observation memberships and
each membership's own measured SNR. For receiver r, let T be the sum of positive
membership weights and W their maximum. Allocate coefficient a = W*w/T to each
membership, and compute S(h) = sum(a * ordinalScore(h, observation, ownSNR)) /
sum(W). Explicit raw mode uses unit membership weights. Canonical joins precede
this budgeting; overlap does not become independent evidence. Include self
terms. Ordinary resolver confusion scoring remains the separate top-cohort,
self-zero contract of ADR-0360.

Aggregate coefficients by eight identities and six SNR bins, requiring at most
432 Score calls for nine hypotheses. Use deterministic order and compensated
sums. Missing/conflicted/out-of-domain SNR on a positive-weight scoring
observation, unavailable opposing score,
nonfinite arithmetic or zero positive total makes all B unavailable. No partial
scores or neutral winning value can authorize action.

Set tau = 1e-9 * max(1, max(abs(S(h)))) over every hypothesis. Only a unique best
beating every opponent by strictly more than tau can act. KEEP, ties and an
ineligible best abstain. Eligibility is surviving C nomination authority;
intersect temporal permission after selecting the full-population winner. The
selected eligible identity receives one ordinary gate trial. Gate or replacement
failure does not authorize a runner-up or a C fallback.

This weighted ordinal compatibility is descriptive. It is not a normalized
string likelihood, Bayes factor, independent-receiver odds or RF posterior.

### Evaluation and operations

Preserve the exact v1 sixteen-cell grid and evaluation admission. Explicit v2
contains C/B at 150 Hz, 60 seconds and distances [1,2,3]. Run active development
only on exposed November 7/14, with disabled full-stream parity on all four
exposed dates. V2 has no confirmation selection or eligibility verdict. Legacy
activation readers continue to refuse experimental artifacts.

Reuse existing capture, artifact, evaluator and exact stability/reference
owners. Preserve all focal input IDs, actual mutation and final outcomes,
loss/retarget/retime unions, 15-minute/three-row/500-Hz stability and R1/R2
contradictions/unknowns. Retain the 4-GiB evidence/assets cap, finite file
inventory, separately reported scratch and COMPLETE-last failure behavior.

Production activation, deployment and resource qualification remain separate.
Existing reference floors and failed resource qualification are unchanged and
unwaived. Disabling the new boolean stops additional live C assessments through
the normal configuration lifecycle; no data migration or model installation is
introduced.

## Alternatives And Consequences

- Retaining focal-only ordinal ranking would preserve the measured lack of
  applied-output contribution; B instead compares common receiver evidence and
  can reject additions. It may still provide no useful increment.
- Relaxing ordinary gates would change the retained behavior and risk policy.
  C expands supported proposals while leaving those gates in force.
- History-only strings could recover systematic busts but lack the owner's
  required current matching report. They gain no active authority here.
- Whole-bucket voting retains unresolved cochannel blocking. C removes only
  directly witnessed exclusions, preserving ambiguous dissent at the cost of
  missed repairs. Mistaken association remains possible.
