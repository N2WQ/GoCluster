## Four sequential implementation units

### 1. Shared selective rescue C

5,120 raw incidences, 16 components, unique attachment within 100 Hz and
pairwise exclusion backed by shared receiver witnesses. The new development
preset uses a 150-Hz gap and 60-second witness allowance. Preserve unknown,
spanning and otherwise unresolved dissent and existing canonical joins.

Permit current supported nominees through configured CW correction distance
three, intersected with the global finite maximum; a global zero permits none
and a negative global maximum does not remove this new method's cap of three.
Retain distance-three report/confidence restrictions and all ordinary gates.
No invented strings, report units, history-only authority or forced publication.

For each nominee, consider all retained identities, including non-nominees.
A credible competing identity has at least two distinct positive-weight
receiver labels in weighted mode, or two labels in explicit raw mode. If that
identity is unusable (unsupported alphabet or canonical length over 32), abstain
without truncating it or running long-string distance work. Otherwise decline
when it is not canonical/family-related and its existing primary-gate distance
from rival observation to nominee exceeds the active correction cap. This
detects that explicit pattern; it does not rule out every competing station.
Related identities remain numerical dissent. Return owned scalar results;
borrowed canonical views remain inside a request-local implementation seam.

### 3. Experimental receiver-compatibility B

Implement a shared pure replay comparison against C using the existing frozen
ordinal asset, without refitting. Consider every retained observed identity,
including zero-weight and non-nominee opponents, plus incoming KEEP (deduplicated).
At most eight observed identities and nine hypotheses are admitted; overflow
abstains without truncation. Every hypothesis sees the same positive-weight
canonical observations and each observation's own measured SNR. Missing,
conflicting/out-of-domain SNR, unavailable opposing score, nonfinite arithmetic
or zero positive total makes B unavailable and preserves the ordinary result.

For receiver r, let T be the sum of its positive membership weights and W their
maximum. Allocate coefficient `a = W*w/T` to each membership. In raw mode use
unit weights. Thus each receiver contributes W in total despite cross-identity
overlap. Compute the weighted mean of existing hypothesis-to-observation ordinal
scores with denominator sum(W). Existing joins first remove source/alias copies.
Zero-weight rows remain structural dissent and their identities remain opponents.
This is descriptive compatibility, not likelihood, independent odds or posterior.

Aggregate coefficients into eight identity by six SNR-bin cells before scoring:
at most 432 distinct Score calls, with the inherited 5,120-incidence bound.
Use deterministic canonical order and compensated score sums. Define numerical
deadband `tau = 1e-9 * max(1, max(abs(mean scores)))`; only a unique winner
exceeding every opponent by more than tau can act. KEEP, ties or an ineligible
winner abstain. C disqualification cannot remove an opposing hypothesis. An
eligible selected winner alone reaches unchanged gates; gate failure does not
authorize a lower-ranked hypothesis. No retained view, cache or background work.
