## Selected policies and scientific limits

- Preserve the narrow guard's strict floating-point comparison. Use the closest
  eligible cross-candidate frequency pair; retain its other support, gap,
  ratio, overlap, distance and family conditions. Reconcile contradictory YAML
  documentation, without changing values/defaults or adding a tolerance.
- Retain the newest observation per receiver, candidate and bucket. Conflicting
  equal-time frequencies require correction abstention until superseded or
  expired. Count that receiver once. Different bucket frequency witnesses remain
  available even when support is deduplicated.
- FT eligibility ends at the earlier quiet-gap or hard expiry. Arrivals exactly
  at expiry or later start a new burst.
- Receiver incidence may overlap: a receiver can support competing candidates.
  Denominators count the receiver union once; each candidate counts its own
  distinct supporters. Shares need not sum to 100 percent.
- Preserve receiver normalization, reliability/admission weights, comparability
  rules, key-wide recency windows, thresholds, hysteresis, configured fallback
  actions, temporal scoring/start-candidate interpretation and output formats.
- Fresh means latest published retained evidence, filtered at release time. It
  does not imply an ingestion barrier or source RF event-time processing.
- **Fresh evidence within the existing key/window does not prove that it belongs
  to the original transmission episode. Wider signal association is deferred.**
- A separate non-steered scientific reviewer independently derived accounting,
  eligibility, frequency-boundary and identity-attribution vectors. Read-only
  design and falsifiability reviews also occurred; lead owns dispositions.
- Independent RF truth and calibration evidence remain missing. These repairs
  establish evidence integrity, not field accuracy or calibrated probability.
  No production capacity or measured field improvement claim is authorized.
- Binary64 storage can make nominal decimal 300 Hz equality compare inside at
  14 MHz and outside at 7 MHz. Preserve that arithmetic and document its limit;
  do not infer RF precision from numerical representation.

### P1: distinct receiver accounting and immutable evidence

Resolver-owned bounded current records carry receiver, candidate, timestamp,
frequency metadata and existing weight. Publish immutable evidence for shared
selection. Union support across admitted buckets, retain losing/third-candidate
evidence, keep existing neighborhood admission/comparability rails. A losing
candidate inside an admitted bucket may be ineligible as a replacement while
its receiver evidence still belongs in the denominator. Older reports cannot
overwrite newer receiver metadata; derived timestamps/eviction agree with the
retained records.

No independent history registry. Couple records/publication to existing key,
candidate and receiver bounds/cleanup. Compact retained temporal scoring data
so pending requests do not pin whole receiver-evidence generations. Establish
accounting, retention, publication and lookup costs before P2 begins.

Checks: duplicate receivers across buckets; dissent and third candidates;
overlapping memberships; unequal weights; ordering/equal-time metadata;
expiry/caps/churn; immutable publication/concurrent lookup; temporal compaction;
delivered output showing manufactured corroboration and confidence disappear.

### P2: binding ambiguity

Qualifying exact/neighborhood protection survives selectors, temporal override,
stabilizer retry and configured fallback. Reevaluate when eligible population
changes; no permanent latch. Closest eligible frequency pair supplies the narrow
predicate; equal-time frequency conflicts abstain. Keep other guards and strict
inequality, values/defaults, family/distance behavior. Reconcile pipeline YAML
comment and affected operator/support guidance.

Checks: exact split through neighborhood and temporal; report permutations for
identical retained eligible populations; fresh legitimate resolution; frequency,
support, ratio, overlap, distance/family boundaries; stored floating-point
threshold examples; actual output identity/conservative glyph.

### P3: fresh temporal authority and truthful counters

At runtime/replay release, filter latest published evidence using actual release
clock and existing key-wide window (lower cutoff inclusive, future excluded),
recompute support/confidence/ambiguity and run ordinary correction gates.
Saved support cannot authorize correction. No shadow older record resurrection,
read-side hysteresis advancement, new ingestion barrier/RPC or score tuning.
Configured fallback remains applicable; fresh ordinary resolver selection can
recover qualifying newer evidence without resetting waits.

Distinguish proposal, accepted identity mutation and attempted handoff. Committed
corrections and confirmed path-switch history follow accepted mutations only:
rejected/unchanged proposals update neither. Retries cannot double-count
acceptance. Bound confirmed per-key history by configured temporal key capacity,
evict deterministically and expose cardinality/evictions. Result markers belong
to bounded request/output state and are consumed once. Handoff is not receipt.

Preserve replay EOF clock, but force retirement selects all pending requests,
including deadlines beyond the 24-hour EOF offset. Expired evidence at that
clock cannot authorize correction. Equivalent-time/evidence parity only; EOF
clock redesign is deferred.

Checks: expired/evicted/future/contradictory evidence; newly sufficient/settled
support; fresh split; gates/CTY; fallback, overflow, bypass and forced retirement;
early output rejection; confirmed-history cap/churn; no duplicate counters;
runtime/replay and actual output recovery/abstention.

### P4: FT expiry on admission

Retire all groups already due at arrival clock before adding incoming reports;
use existing heap and shared release path, due/sequence order and exactly-once
retirement. No retired-group backlog or additional worker. Preserve supported
modes, caps, thresholds, history floor and arrival-time grouping semantics.

Checks: delayed drain; quiet/hard expiry equality and adjacent times; duplicate
receivers, modes/nondefault settings, capacity/unrelated heads and stale heap
churn; actual output showing late receivers do not upgrade old bursts.

### P5: emitted-identity confidence

Derive percentage/availability from final normalized emitted call on every
outcome. Rejected B with retained A keeps rejection marker and A's confidence;
successful correction gets winner confidence. Clear unavailable support under
existing validity conventions. No protocol/archive schema change.

Checks: equal-weight retained A one of three = 33 percent, rejected B two of
three = 66 percent; unequal weights use A's weighted share. Cover successful,
gate/invalid/CTY rejection, broadcast/suppress, split, unchanged/missing evidence,
immediate/temporal/retry outcomes and bypass controls; actual consumer output.
