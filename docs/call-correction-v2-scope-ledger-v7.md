## Objective and owner decisions

Build and run a complete **replay-only correction method** that can nominate
currently supported nearby calls using frequency evidence and Bayesian-derived
ranking. Determine whether it produces more applied corrections with an
unchanged or better exact follow-on stability fraction. Preserve the complete
paired outcomes and existing independent-reference requirements. The experiment
may find no gain, or find that frequency-based nomination helps without Bayes.

The owner explicitly selected signal-specific gate populations when separation
is supported, retaining ambiguous evidence as dissent and other guard/history/
license requirements. Active separation must have frequency evidence;
time-gap-only proposals remain offline. A replacement needs a current matching
report. Earlier decisions preserve aggressiveness with acknowledged genuine-
neighbor risk, each receiver's own SNR, equal-time conflicts as unknown,
cross-receiver/mode harmonic comparisons, and deferred CPU/heap optimization.

This scope develops and evaluates the method without wiring it into live
delivery. If it succeeds, freeze its actual behavior, assets and results for a
subsequent live-integration ledger. That boundary permits concrete method work
while preventing an experimental threshold or unqualified model from becoming
a production setting. Thursday production readiness is not established here.

## Frequency-backed proposal contract

Use existing immutable publications and configured neighborhood radius, at most
five keys, 80 raw candidate slots and 5,120 raw receiver incidences. Preserve
source frequency witnesses before canonical merging; a favorable merged
frequency cannot conceal another source observation. No ingestion barrier,
extra Step/drain, background worker or reconstructed archive history is allowed.

The study's operational association family is explicit:

1. Keep all current raw observations and their source keys. Known positive,
   nonconflicted frequencies form sorted reported-frequency components; a new
   component starts at an adjacent frequency gap at least the candidate's
   declared gap threshold. Spelling, prefix, CTY estimate, SNR and favorable
   ranking scores do not partition the observations.
2. Associate the focal frequency only when exactly one component is within
   100 Hz of an actual reported frequency in that component. The focal report
   is a query, not an invented receiver vote. No unique association means
   ordinary fallback.
3. Each proposed separation requires at least two distinct receiver labels
   represented in each relevant component, plus at least two distinct shared
   receiver labels witnessing the two components within the declared witness
   time separation. Compare actual per-receiver frequencies/times; repeated
   rows or aliases do not inflate the witness count. This is an operational
   reported-frequency test, not proof of statistical receiver independence.
4. A component lacking affirmative exclusion evidence remains ambiguous
   dissent. Unknown frequency is not measured zero. Preserve its receiver
   membership in the assessment rather than dropping it. A canonical identity
   spanning competing components is unresolved for the additional path.
   More than 16 components makes additional assessment unavailable; do not
   truncate components to obtain an apparent success.
5. Preserve global known-loss protection, ordinary protected split/uncertain
   authority, pending-transition and applicable raw-tie safeguards. An unknown
   frequency conflict cannot be deleted to regain authority. A nominee must
   have current eligible support in an owner-published settled source and
   remain replacement-eligible under canonical/slash policy. No new owner
   settlement or history-only candidate gains authority here.
6. Candidate-to-subject mode-aware distance/family limits and existing gate
   requirements remain. A supported separated proposal may come from a source
   whose unrelated bucket winner prevented ordinary neighborhood admission;
   the explicit frequency evidence supplies this new proposal's association
   authority. The ordinary neighborhood admission/fallback path remains intact.

Derive signal-specific gate counts with the existing canonical receiver-union,
weight and alias semantics over the associated component plus unresolved
dissent. A receiver counts once in a population, not once per spelling or key.
No fractional or fabricated receiver units are introduced. Unavailable or mixed
support bases cannot regain a numerical confidence by substituting raw counts.

Bound additional proposals to eight complete gate trials per incoming decision.
Cheap current-match, authority, association and distance checks precede ranking
and history queries. Score the bounded current candidates before choosing the
eight trials; expose a beam-limit outcome rather than claiming every candidate
was fully gated. Each gate retains its existing per-call history-query semantics;
no new shared history snapshot/cache or weaker validation floor is added.
The additional path accepts only canonical ASCII alphabet members of at most
32 characters, for both ranking variants. Other calls retain ordinary handling;
they are not truncated or rejected by the parser. Subject-equal identities and
alias-only display changes are not additional repair trials.

These numerical association rules are predeclared experiment conventions.
Neither the old 0.3 kHz ambiguity setting nor current witnesses calibrate them
as RF-safe thresholds. The study must falsify them with the counterexamples and
complete outcomes below; no active production threshold is selected by this scope.

## Bayesian-derived ordinal nomination

The model target is the distribution of **algorithmically assigned edit events
between accepted canonical reports and the frozen strong-agree R1/R2 proxy**,
by CW mode and focal measured-SNR bin. It is not a physical error-event label
or probability that a transmitted call is correct.

Use the existing six SNR intervals and explicit measured/missing distinction.
The focal observation and its own SNR are the same evidence for every compared
candidate. Do not multiply receiver likelihoods, use another call's SNR, or add
overlapping history/current support as an independent prior. Current support
and Custom SCP continue to serve the ordinary gates. Candidate ranking uses
no learned call-popularity prior.

Count every exact match as well as substitutions/deletions/insertions. Use the
declared unit-edit suffix alignment, walking forward with match, substitution,
deletion, insertion precedence on equal cost. Preserve full pairs and reconcile
the preliminary unstratified operation/opportunity totals before fitting;
alias-only normalization is not a decoder-error label.
Fit in **proxy reference to observed subject** direction; score in **hypothesized
candidate to focal observation** direction. Do not reverse both and accept
their self-consistency as an oracle.

For the 37-symbol canonical alphabet (A–Z, 0–9, slash), use a fixed symmetric
Dirichlet prior with **total mass one** over the 38 per-source-character
emission/deletion outcomes. Its posterior mean is
`(event_count + 1/38) / (source_character_opportunities + 1)`.
Insertion-symbol probabilities use total prior mass one over 37 symbols.
Gap insertion continuation has a fixed Beta(1,1) working prior:
`q = (insertions + 1) / (insertions + gap_stops + 2)`.
Each source call contributes `length + 1` gap stops. These are Bayesian-smoothed
event estimates under the stated working event model; repeated/selected RBN
reports do not establish calibrated posterior uncertainty.

The ordinal score is the log weight of that one declared alignment: its
emission/deletion terms, inserted-symbol and continuation terms, and one
`log(1-q)` stop per source gap. **Do not call this the marginal probability of
the observed string or a Bayes factor.** Alternative edit paths and accepted-
report selection can require additional normalization. Candidate softmax does
not repair that issue. No score becomes a public confidence percentage.

Missing or out-of-domain focal SNR, unsupported modes, or fewer than 50 source-
character opportunities for any candidate character in its CW/SNR cell, or
fewer than 50 gap stops in that cell, make the optional score unavailable.
The 50 floor is not a requirement for 50 errors or 50 occurrences of each
inserted symbol: zero-error rows and unseen insertion symbols have declared
prior contributions. Aggregate-bin coverage cannot substitute for an unexposed
source character. If any compared eligible
candidate lacks its required score, use the geometry-only ordering for the
whole decision, retaining every candidate and its support. Do not award an
unavailable candidate a zero log score against negative measured scores.
Geometry-only ordering is existing weighted support, raw support, then lexical
identity. Bayesian-score ties use that same deterministic ordering.

Training uses only exposed November 7/14 inputs and the frozen reference
procedure; no November 17+ observations, future confirmation result or withheld
receiver feature enters the predictor. Repeated-observation/episode dependence,
reference selection, sparse bins, unseen symbols and unscored denominators stay
explicit. No credible interval or calibrated RF-probability claim is authorized.
