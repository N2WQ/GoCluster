## Architecture and preserved contracts

The resolver remains the single mutation owner. Existing raw admission caps,
expiry, eviction, loss and retirement own the added receiver metadata. No second
SNR registry, lifetime pair cache, blocking ingest barrier, new persistence schema
or global historical snapshot is authorized.

Confusion hypotheses AND observations remain restricted to the top cohort tied
in raw AND weighted support. Each receiver supplies its own available in-domain
SNR and weight; missing/conflicted/out-of-domain measurements omit only the
optional model contribution. Support/denominators survive. Zero weights must not
gain invented per-receiver units. Equivalent canonical identities have zero
self-edit contribution. Preserve the existing ranking-potential meaning and
positive-weight tie ordering; do not clamp, impose strict SNR monotonicity,
normalize by coverage, or add confusion terms/SNR guards to Bayes.

Reuse call-pair alignment and bounded temporary SNR-bin reduction. Partial-fresh
projection recomputes optional ordering from surviving published rows using local
scratch, retaining or revoking only the previously settled semantic identity.
It never settles a new winner or advances owner hysteresis. All-fresh lookup
reuses its immutable generation; Compact discards full receiver generations.
Order-independence claims concern completed eligible evidence at a defined clock
and evaluation state, not arbitrary scheduling of already emitted outputs.

Raw owner entries retain their existing spelling/receiver caps. Canonical groups
union receivers across equivalent VoteKeys and may contain more than 64 receivers;
do not silently recap them. Preserve raw SNR/frequency provenance and contradictory
witnesses. Choose an eligible observed display by support, recency, then lexical
order; do not invent an observed canonical spelling. Owner/pending/temporal state,
membership, model comparisons and accounting use semantic identity consistently.
Fresh release resolves the identity to a current observed display before CTY,
licensing and actual mutation. Alias/display changes alone do not count as new
corrections, switches or hysteresis transitions. Final-alias removal revokes
authority. Bare-call dissent remains in the denominator; slash precedence limits
eligibility and does not waive ordinary gates. Preserve nomination from admitted
settled populations, not a new winners-only rule.

Keep current RF-association assumptions, full eligible receiver union, mode-aware
distance, nonblocking asynchronous ingestion, ordinary ambiguity/edit-neighbor
guards, license checks, original-peer/archive contracts, temporal additive
objective, initial-held-request interpretation, waits, smoothing and transitions.
Many bare-call distance-1 ties remain protected before Bayes; ordinary one-short
distance-1 cases are already served by recent +1. Demonstrate consumer reachability
before tuning and attribute S2-S4 gains separately from Bayesian increments.

## Archive qualification and activation

Freeze episode/association/reference rules, training/tuning/holdout populations,
future-reference horizons, embargoes and acceptance criteria before final holdout
content inspection. Existing assets trained through November 14 cannot establish
a clean historical holdout on their own training dates. Earlier chronological
holdouts require isolated training-only asset reconstruction. No automatic
runtime model-asset installation is authorized.

Use prespecified disjoint-receiver and strictly-later reference controls. Exclude
focal receiver aliases/repeats and avoid shared predictor/reference observations.
Withheld receivers must be absent from prediction history/learning as well as
local votes. Silence and insufficient evidence remain unknown, not false positives.
Disclose receiver/date/episode dependence, largest-ZIP selection bias, training
exposure, label coverage, and any reduced-input qualification population. Full-input
shadow/capacity results cannot inherit independent accuracy from a reduced-input
reference study. Independent RF-truth oracle is missing.

Compare unchanged shipped baseline, repaired pipeline/assets with Bayes off,
and the identical repaired pipeline/assets with Bayes on. Preserve identical raw
IDs and frozen references within each comparison, including changed eligibility,
holds, suppression, retention and unknowns. No intersection-only denominator.

At most 16 predeclared COMPLETE tuning candidates may be evaluated. Each includes
one parameter vector AND the entire enabled mode/distance map. Keep existing
smoothing, caps, distance weights, history minimums, validation and guard
qualifications. Select/freeze one complete candidate/assets/map before untouched
holdout. Do not combine cell winners from different configurations.

For EACH newly active mode x distance cell require:

- At least 200 distinct additional normalized-identity correction episodes,
  jointly assessable by both reference controls.
- At least 50 qualifying episodes on each of two untouched UTC dates.
- Additional reference-supported corrections on each date under both controls.
- No new strong-reference-disagreeing rename under either control.
- At least 80 percent reference coverage of ALL incremental episode IDs,
  independently for each control.

Repeated receipts, unchanged outputs and holds do not inflate episodes. A strong
contradiction cannot be relabeled unknown. These are engineering floors, not RF
accuracy guarantees. All active cells must pass in the exact stateful combination.
Any active-cell failure selects WHOLE-Bayes-off fallback. Changed masks/configs
need fresh untouched confirmation; no same-holdout adaptive selection.

One optional bounded setting, `bayes_bonus.enabled_distances_by_mode`, maps modes
to subsets of [1, 2]. Omission preserves legacy eligibility; explicit {} enables
no cells; a mode's [] enables no distances; master false overrides it. Reject null,
unsupported modes/distances and normalized duplicates. Preserve omitted versus
explicit-empty through YAML and documented direct-Go/mapping states. Use one
effective eligibility contract across live/replay/evaluator, diagnostics and both
history-capacity validators. Capacity means structurally selected cells permitted
by MaxEditDistance: score-unreachable enabled paths still query history. Remove
only unused Bayes requirements; preserve recent +1 (including distance-zero slash),
truncation, stabilizer and other consumers. Legacy old thresholds remain bootable.
Search bounds are not new blanket startup rejection rules. Delivered first-party
configuration explicitly selects only the exact qualified combination; none means
Bayes inactive. Arbitrary operator/legacy configurations are not qualification
certificates.
